# Product Requirement Document (PRD): Gurukrupa Paint Website

## 1. Product Overview
*   **Product Name:** Gurukrupa Paint Official Website
*   **Type:** Business Portfolio + Lead Generation
*   **Industry:** Painting & Home Improvement
*   **Target Locations:** Mumbai, Thane, Maharashtra
*   **Established:** 1992
*   **Positioning:** Premium painting contractor

## 2. Goals
*   Generate qualified leads (WhatsApp, Calls, Forms).
*   Build a premium brand image.
*   Showcase a high-quality portfolio.
*   Optimize for local SEO (Mumbai/Thane).

## 3. Key Features
*   **UI/UX:** Premium Glassmorphism, fully responsive, smooth scroll (Framer Motion style).
*   **Navigation:** Floating centered navbar with blur effect.
*   **Lead Gen:** Formspree integration, Click-to-call, WhatsApp floating button with pulse animation.
*   **Content:** Gallery with lightbox, Google Maps embed.

## 4. Proposed Screen List
1.  **Home Page (`/`):** Hero with glassmorphism, key services overview, "Since 1992" trust badge, testimonials, and primary CTA.
2.  **Services Overview (`/services`):** Detailed grid of offerings (Interior, Exterior, House Painting, Commercial).
3.  **Project Gallery (`/gallery`):** High-fidelity grid of completed projects with categories.
4.  **About & Trust (`/about`):** Company history, team expertise, and service area map.
5.  **Contact & Lead Capture (`/contact`):** Full contact form, direct contact details, and embedded Google Map.

## 5. Design System Direction
*   **Style:** Modern Glassmorphism (blur, transparency, thin borders).
*   **Colors:** Soft gradients with blue and purple tones, balanced with clean whites/light grays.
*   **Typography:** Poppins (Sans-serif) for a clean, modern feel.
*   **Components:** Rounded corners, glass cards, animated buttons.
